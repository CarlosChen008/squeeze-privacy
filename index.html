Privacy Policy — Squeeze

Last updated: 2026-09-20

Squeeze (“the app”, “we”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how your information is handled when you use the app.

Summary

Squeeze does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Your photos and videos are processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded.

Information We Collect

None. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

Your Photos and Videos

  • The app requests access to your photos/videos only to display and compress them.
  • All processing happens locally on your device.
  • Your media is never transmitted to us or to any third party.
  • When you compress an item, the app replaces the original file in place, and only after you approve Android’s system write-request dialog.

Permissions

  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_MEDIA_VIDEO (or READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on older Android versions): to browse your gallery.
  • Writing/modifying files: performed through Android’s system write-request dialog. The app cannot modify your files without your explicit approval each time.

Internet and Third Parties

  • The app does not upload your media and does not request internet access.
  • In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. Google may process purchase-related information under Google’s own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Data Retention and Deletion

We collect no data, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete. App settings (such as your chosen quality) are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

Children’s Privacy

The app is not directed to children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted at this URL with a new “last updated” date.

隐私政策 — 嗖缩（Squeeze）

最后更新：2026-09-20

嗖缩（下称“本应用”）尊重你的隐私。本政策说明你在使用本应用时，相关信息是如何被处理的。

概要

嗖缩不收集、不存储、不共享任何个人数据。你的照片和视频全部在本机处理，绝不上传。

我们收集的信息

无。本应用没有账号、没有统计、没有广告，也不做任何追踪。

你的照片与视频

  • 本应用申请访问照片/视频，仅用于展示和压缩。
  • 所有处理都在你的设备本地完成。
  • 你的媒体文件绝不会传输给我们或任何第三方。
  • 压缩时，本应用会在你通过 Android 系统写回授权弹窗后，才原地替换原文件。

权限说明

  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_MEDIA_VIDEO （旧版本 Android 为 READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE）：用于浏览相册。
  • 写入/修改文件：通过 Android 系统写回授权弹窗完成。每一次都必须经你明确同意， 本应用才能修改你的文件。

网络与第三方

  • 本应用不上传你的媒体，也不申请联网权限。
  • 应用内购买由 Google Play 处理。Google 可能依据其自身隐私政策处理与购买相关的信息： https://policies.google.com/privacy。

数据留存与删除

我们不收集任何数据，因此没有需要留存或删除的内容。应用设置（如你选择的质量档位） 仅保存在你的设备本地，卸载应用即被清除。

儿童隐私

本应用不面向儿童，也不收集任何人的数据。

政策变更

我们可能更新本政策。更新后会发布在本网址，并标注新的“最后更新”日期。
